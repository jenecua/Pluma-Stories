Blood must pay

Author: R.J.C.

Chapter 1: Blood Debt

        
        The Reyes family was an ordinary family.
        
        They lived in a quiet neighborhood on the outskirts of the city. Their house wasn't large, but it was comfortable enough for the five people who called it home.
        
        Every morning, Eduardo Reyes left for work before sunrise. His wife, Marissa, prepared breakfast while their children rushed to get ready for school.
        
        They had no enemies.
        
        No debts.
        
        No dark secrets.
        
        At least, none that they knew of.
        
        
        On a cold Monday evening, the family gathered around the dinner table as they always did. Laughter filled the room.
        
        Their youngest son was talking about a school project while his older sister complained about an upcoming exam. For a brief moment, everything seemed normal. Then the doorbell rang.
        
        Everyone fell silent.
        
        "Are we expecting someone?" Marissa asked.
        
        Eduardo shook his head.
        
        "No."
        
        He rose from his chair and walked toward the front door. Looking through the peephole, he saw a man wearing a black coat standing outside. The stranger's face was hidden beneath a hood.
        
        "Can I help you?" Eduardo asked through the door.
        
        No response. The stranger simply stood there. A strange feeling crept into Eduardo's chest. He slowly turned the lock. The moment the door opened, the stranger smiled. Then he handed Eduardo an old
        
        photograph. Confused, Eduardo examined it. The photograph was faded and yellow with age. It showed a young man standing beside another man whose face had been scratched out.
        
        Written on the back were three words: Blood must pay. Eduardo's hands trembled. "I think you've got the wrong house," he said.
        
        The stranger's smile widened. "No," he replied softly. "I finally found the right one.
        
        Before Eduardo could react, the stranger turned and walked away into the darkness. The family contacted the police that night. The officers dismissed it as a prank and advised them not to worry.
        
        For a few days, life returned to normal. Then the killings began. Three blocks away, an elderly woman was found dead in her home. The next night, a man was discovered murdered in an abandoned alley.
        
        A week later, another victim appeared. Each victim shared something unusual. All of them were somehow connected to the Reyes family. The police couldn't explain it. Neither could the family.
        
        Fear slowly settled into their home.
        
        One evening, Marissa decided to search through old family records stored in their attic. She hoped to find something, anything, that could explain the photograph. Hours passed as she dug through dusty
        
        boxes and forgotten documents. Then she found an old journal. The journal belonged to their ancestor, Gabriel Reyes. Marissa opened it carefully.
        
        Most of the pages contained ordinary entries about farming and daily life. But near the end, she found a passage that made her blood run cold.
        
        "I buried him where no one would ever find him." Marissa's hands shook. She read the next line. "If his family seeks justice, let them search forever." The journal slipped from her grasp. Her heart pounded.
        
        For more than a century, the Reyes family had lived peacefully, unaware of what one man had done.
        
        Gabriel Reyes had murdered someone. And now, it seemed that someone had finally come to collect the debt. Downstairs, the doorbell rang again.
        
        Slowly.
        
        Once.
        
        Twice.
        
        Three times.
        
        Marissa stared toward the attic stairs. A terrible feeling settled in her stomach. Somewhere beyond the front door, someone was waiting. And this time, they weren't looking for answers.
        
        They were looking for the next member of the Reyes bloodline.
