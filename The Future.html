The Future

Author: R.J.C.

Chapter 1: Where Am I?


        Exhaustion finally caught up with me after a long day at work. The moment I got home, I collapsed into bed and drifted into a deep sleep. In my dream, I was enjoying a sunny afternoon on a beautiful
        
        white-sand beach. The sun warmed my skin as I lounged by the shore, wearing nothing but swimming trunks and sunglasses. A glass of fresh coconut juice rested in my hand, while a plate of delicious
        
        baked scallops sat beside me. "What a life," I thought contentedly.

        Then, suddenly, a loud noise shattered the peaceful scene. BANG! BANG! BANG! It sounded like someone was pounding on a door.
        
        I opened my eyes. Instead of the beach, I found myself lying on a bed inside an unfamiliar room. "Is this another dream?" The banging continued, growing louder and more impatient.

        No. This felt too real. "Wake up, B2135! Open your door for inspection!" a man shouted from the other side. I sat up abruptly, my mind still clouded with sleep.
        
        "Who are you?" I called out. "Open the door, or I'll break it down! This is an inspection!" What the hell was happening? Just moments ago,
        
        I had come home from work and fallen asleep. I had been dreaming about a vacation on a beautiful beach.

        Now, I was in a strange room, and some man was threatening to break down my door. "What is going on?" I tried to make sense of my surroundings, but nothing seemed familiar. "I warn you, B2135!"
        
        the man barked. "I'll count to three. If you don't open this door, you won't get any food until dinner!" Panic crept through me. Who was this man? Why was he calling me B2135? And what would happen
        
        if I opened the door?

        Despite my fear, I forced myself out of bed and approached the door. My heart pounded so hard that I could feel it in my chest. My hand hovered near the handle, trembling slightly.

        Then, just as I was about to open it, a woman's voice reached me from the other side. "Damian, it's me. Please open the door. Don't be afraid."
        
        I froze. Damian? Was she talking to me? I didn't know who she was, but there was something strangely comforting about her voice. It was gentle and soothing, and somehow, it eased the tension gripping
        
        my chest. Taking a deep breath, I finally gathered the courage to open the door.
        
        The moment it swung open, a beautiful woman stood before me. She had fair skin, almond-shaped eyes, a pointed nose, cherry-red lips, and long, wavy hair that framed her face.

        Before I could say a word, she stepped forward and wrapped her arms around me. I stiffened in surprise. "Who is she? Why is she hugging me?"
        
        Her body was warm, and the moment she embraced me, the tension I'd been holding inside seemed to melt away. For reasons I couldn't explain, I didn't push her away.
