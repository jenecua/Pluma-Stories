The Accident

Author: R.J.C.

Chapter 1: The Boy

        
        It was a hot, sunny morning when Jimmy’s truck suddenly lost its brakes and collided with a car. He quickly got off the truck to check, but froze. His knees weakened, and tears streamed from his eyes.
        
        The car was a total wreck. He knew he would be jailed for what had happened.
        
        Suddenly, he heard the voice of a child crying for help. Jimmy hurried to the car and forcefully opened the door to check the victims.
        
        Inside, he found a boy covered in blood. He pulled the child out and called for an ambulance. Moments later, Jimmy fainted from the trauma. When he woke up, he was lying in a hospital bed in the ward.
        
        He called for a nurse and asked what had happened to the victims, especially the boy he had rescued. The nurse told him to rest and avoid anything that might cause stress.

        But Jimmy yelled at the nurse, insisting on finding the boy he had saved from the accident. The nurses restrained him and injected a sedative to calm him, putting him back to sleep.
        
        That night, Jimmy woke up and saw the boy standing near the door. The child suddenly ran into the hallway. Jimmy quickly grabbed his IV line, jumped out of bed,
        
        and chased after him.
        
        But when he reached the hallway, people stared at him in confusion. Nurses held him back and pulled him inside the ward. “Did you see a boy? He was just standing there and then ran into the hallway!”
        
        Jimmy said, pointing at the door.
        
        “Sir, please relax,” replied the nurse. “We didn’t see any boy. I think you need to rest so you can recover.” But Jimmy knew what he saw. He was certain the boy was alive — and healthy.

        The nurses began to think Jimmy had lost his mind, perhaps from bumping his head. The next day, the doctor checked on Jimmy and asked if he felt any pain in his body. Jimmy replied that he felt none.
        
        Then the doctor asked about the boy. The nurses exchanged uneasy glances. “Which boy?” the doctor asked.

        Jimmy explained that he had saved a boy from the accident. The doctor looked at him calmly and said, “Jimmy, you were the only person in that accident.
        
        There were no collisions. You simply lost control of the truck and hit a wall. No one else was hurt—only you.”

        Again, the nurses glanced at each other, their silence heavy with something unspoken. Jimmy felt certain they were hiding the truth from him.
        
        “I don’t feel any pain,” Jimmy insisted. “I want to leave this hospital as soon as possible.”
