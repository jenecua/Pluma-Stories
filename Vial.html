Vial

Author: R.J.C.

Chapter 1: Exchanged


        An old man ran down the middle of the highway while three men chased him. Cars honked angrily as they swerved around them.
        
        Nathalie stepped out of a café just as the old man accidentally bumped into her. The vial he was holding slipped from his hand and rolled across the pavement. At the same time, the vial Nathalie had
        
        purchased earlier also fell and rolled away.
        
        Nathalie quickly picked up a vial and placed it inside her bag. The old man grabbed the other vial and continued running.
        
        Neither of them realized that the vials had been switched.
        
        The old man entered a nearby building and rushed to the rooftop. Cornered by the three pursuers, he took out the vial, drank its contents, and leaped from the building.
        
        Moments later, he was dead.
        
        Meanwhile, Nathalie continued walking home, occasionally glancing at the vial inside her bag.
        
        When she arrived home, she ate dinner and examined the vial. Believing it to be the medicine prescribed by her doctor, she injected it into her arm.
        
        A minute later, she collapsed.
        
        An hour later, Nathalie suddenly woke up. Feeling disoriented, she immediately went to see her doctor.
        
        "Doctor, is it possible to pass out after injecting the vial?" Nathalie asked.
        
        The doctor frowned.
        
        "No. You should be fine. There are no side effects that would cause you to lose consciousness," he replied.
        
        "Then why did I collapse?" Nathalie asked worriedly.
        
        The doctor thought for a moment.
        
        "I think we should run another laboratory test and review your previous results. The substance is already in your system, so we need to make sure everything is normal."
        
        Nathalie nodded.
        
        The doctor ordered a new series of tests. After several hours, the results arrived.
        
        As he examined the reports, his eyes widened in disbelief.
        
        It was impossible.
        
        Nathalie had been diagnosed with an incurable illness. Just a day earlier, she had been told she had only a month left to live.
        
        But the new test results showed something completely different.
        
        She was healthy.
        
        Perfectly healthy.
        
        The doctor immediately informed Nathalie of the results.
        
        Nathalie stared at him in confusion.
        
        "Doctor, are you sure?" she asked.
        
        "One hundred percent sure," the doctor replied. "These are the results of your laboratory tests. Whatever was affecting your body is completely gone."
        
        Nathalie looked down at the report, unable to believe what she was seeing.
        
        Only yesterday, she had been preparing for death.
        
        Now, according to every test in front of her, she was perfectly healthy.
