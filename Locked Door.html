Locked door

Author: R.J.C.

Chapter 1: Curiosity


        It was summertime, and as usual, we were going to our ancestral house where my aunt lived.
        
        The house was enormous. I could go anywhere I wanted inside the mansion, except for one room.
        
        No one ever opened that door.
        
        When we arrived at my aunt's estate, everything looked the same as always. The vast garden was filled with different kinds of flowers, and a fountain stood proudly at the center. On top of it was a statue
        
        of a man holding a jar, looking as though it had come straight from ancient Rome. My aunt's butler greeted us as soon as we stepped out of the car. Dad handed him the keys while the maids hurried outside
        
        to collect our luggage.

        "Hi! How are you guys? I've missed you for so long!" Aunt Cecilia came running toward us and immediately wrapped me in a hug. I smiled.
        
        "My, you've grown a little," she said, looking me up and down. "And you're still as handsome as ever."
        
        "What about me?" my father asked jokingly. She turned to him and laughed.
        
        "Still as ugly as ever." Dad placed a hand over his chest in mock offense, causing everyone to laugh. Then Aunt Cecilia hugged him as well. "I missed you, brother." Dad gently tapped her head.
        
        "I missed you too." "I'm starving," Dad said. "Don't you have any food prepared?"
        
        Aunt Cecilia released him from the hug and smiled. "Of course. Everything's ready. Come eat with me."
        
        After dinner, I headed toward my room, or rather, the room Aunt Cecilia called mine.

        Before going upstairs, however, I glanced down the hallway. There it was. The room. Unlike the others, its door had a different style. It was made of dark carved hardwood, covered with intricate designs
        
        depicting Roman soldiers and scenes of war. I had been curious about it for as long as I could remember. I desperately wanted to know what was hidden inside. It was the only room in the entire house that
        
        I was forbidden to enter.
        
        That night, I couldn't sleep. Thoughts of the mysterious room filled my mind.
        
        Finally, curiosity got the better of me. I slipped out of bed and quietly made my way downstairs. Like a burglar sneaking through a museum, I carefully avoided making any noise. The hallway was dimly lit.
        
        I stared at the forbidden door.
        
        Then, just as I took a step forward, it suddenly opened. I froze. My father and Aunt Cecilia stepped out from inside. Something about Dad seemed different. The warm and approachable man I knew was gone.
        
        In his place stood someone cold and frightening.

        "Keep his business away from us," Dad said in a serious voice as they walked down the hallway.
        
        "I understand," Aunt Cecilia replied. As I shifted my weight on the stairs, a loud creak echoed through the hallway. Both of them immediately looked up. "What are you doing awake at this hour?"
        
        Dad asked sternly.

        "You're scaring the child, Celion," Aunt Cecilia said with a smile. She walked toward me and gently took my hand.
        
        "Come on, child. Let's get you back to your room. Were you looking for a snack?"
        
        I nodded awkwardly. Without another word, she led me upstairs and tucked me into bed. Then she sat beside me and softly sang a lullaby. Before I knew it, my eyes grew heavy. And this time, it worked.
