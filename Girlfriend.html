Girlfriend

Author: R.J.C.

Chapter 1: What happened?

        
        Jerry was walking to his girlfriend's house, carrying a bouquet of flowers. As he approached, he noticed a police car parked outside.
        
        Confused, he climbed the porch steps and knocked on the door.
        
        "Celine?" he called.
        
        The door opened, revealing Celine's mother. She looked him straight in the eyes but said nothing. Tears streamed down her face.
        
        Without a word, she turned and walked back into the living room.
        
        Jerry slowly entered the house and closed the door behind him. As he followed her, he saw a detective and two police officers interviewing Celine's father.
        
        The detective and the officers turned to look at him.
        
        "He's Celine's boyfriend," her father said. "But they weren't together yesterday. Celine left the house to buy something she had seen online. We don't know where she went."
        
        His voice trembled.

        "This morning, someone called and told us that our daughter had been found dead. We have no idea who could have done this to her. Celine was a kind and loving child."
        
        Jerry felt his stomach drop.
        
        "I'm sorry... what happened?" he asked, unable to believe what he had just heard.
        
        Celine's mother rushed forward and embraced him.
        
        "Celine is gone," she whispered through her tears.
        
        Jerry froze. Tears filled his eyes, and the bouquet slipped from his hand, falling to the floor.
        
        "No..." he muttered.
        
        Unable to accept the truth, he ran upstairs to Celine's bedroom, calling her name over and over.
        
        "Celine! Celine!"

        Downstairs, Celine's mother broke into loud sobs and collapsed into a sitting position on the living room floor. One of the police officers quickly went to her side and helped her onto the couch.
        
        A few minutes later, Jerry came back downstairs. His eyes were red and swollen from crying.
        
        He stopped in front of Celine's parents, struggling to speak.
        
        "I... I want to go home," he said quietly.
        
        Celine's father nodded in understanding.
        
        "Okay, son," he replied softly.
        
        Jerry gave them one last look before turning toward the door.
