He Finally Said It

Author: R.J.C.

Chapter 1: The Moment I Held His Hand

        
        An old man lay sick in bed, a machine monitoring his heart rate as he breathed through an oxygen tank. His wife sat beside him, holding his hand tightly with both of hers,
        
        tears streaming rapidly down her face. And after thirty-eight years, he finally said it.

        Forty Years Ago…
        
        “Yes!” yelled Olivia. “At last! I’m finally a college graduate!” She wore her graduation gown and proudly held her bachelor’s diploma.
        
        Running to her parents, she hugged them tightly. They took a photo together as a keepsake.

        A week later, Olivia was hired by her dream company, in her dream position, with a competitive salary. It was there that she met Charles — a shy, good-looking, well-mannered,
        
        and hard-working man.

        Their manager came up with an idea to make work easier. He suggested forming groups for each project so employees could focus only on their assigned tasks.
        
        Everyone quickly paired off with close colleagues, leaving Olivia and Charles standing alone in the middle.

        “So, I think we’re all settled,” said the manager. “Olivia and Charles, since you’re the last two standing, you’ll be partners.” Olivia glanced at Charles,
        
        but he only bowed his head without looking at her.
        
        The next day, Olivia brought an orange juice to Charles, hoping to start a conversation. But Charles answered all her questions with the fewest words possible.
        
        Olivia grew a little annoyed and returned to her desk, deciding not to talk to him for the rest of the day.

        The following morning, Charles nervously approached Olivia, his hands shaking. He offered her an orange juice and a small strawberry cake. “Are you mad at me?” he asked.
        
        “If you are, I’m sorry. It wasn’t my intention. I’m just not used to talking with someone.”
        
        Olivia smiled, taking the juice and cake. “Apology accepted. Next time, try to make your answers longer. But I’m amazed you knew I was upset, even though you never looked at me once,” she said.
        
        “So… partners?” Olivia asked, reaching out her hand.
        
        Charles looked at her for the first time. “Partners.” he said, smiling.
