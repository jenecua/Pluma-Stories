Scarlet Spoon

Author: R.J.C.

Chapter 1: The Taste Everyone Loved

        
        A new restaurant had become the talk of the city. Scarlet Spoon was always packed.
        
        From morning until midnight, customers lined up outside its doors. People traveled from neighboring towns just to taste its food. No one could explain why it was so good.
        
        The recipes were simple. The ingredients were ordinary. Yet one bite was enough to keep people coming back. Some customers visited every day. Others spent their savings eating there. It was almost
        
        like an addiction.
        
        One rainy evening, food blogger Emma Reyes sat in the crowded restaurant, taking notes. She had come to discover the secret behind Scarlet Spoon's success.
        
        As she tasted the restaurant's famous stew, she understood the hype immediately. It was unlike anything she had ever eaten. Rich. Savory. Unforgettable. But something felt wrong.

        While exploring the building after closing time, Emma noticed a locked door at the end of a narrow hallway.
        
        A strange metallic smell drifted from beneath it. Curious, she peeked through a crack in the door.
        
        Inside, workers in white uniforms poured a dark red liquid into a large steel container. Emma's heartbeat quickened.
        
        The liquid looked disturbingly familiar. One of the workers noticed movement outside.
        
        Emma quickly stepped away and fled the area before anyone could see her. The next morning, she reviewed the photos she had secretly taken.
        
        As she zoomed in on one image, her face turned pale. Attached to the container was a label. Three words. Human Blood Storage. Emma stared at the screen in horror. Suddenly, her phone rang.
        
        An unknown caller.
        
        When she answered, a cold voice whispered: "You shouldn't have seen that." The line went dead.
