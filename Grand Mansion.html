Grand Mansion

Author: R.J.C.

Chapter 1: The Forgotten Legend


        Every child in the kingdom had heard the tale of the Grand Mansion.
        
        For centuries, the mysterious mansion had appeared only in legends. Travelers, adventurers, treasure hunters, and even kings had spent their lives searching for it. Yet none of them ever succeeded.
        
        Some claimed the mansion did not exist. Others swore it was hidden beyond enchanted forests and forgotten mountains. But according to the oldest stories, the Grand Mansion could never be found by
        
        those who sought it. Only those with pure hearts, and those who were not looking for it, could stumble upon its gates.
        
        It was said that within the mansion lived a beautiful queen and a handsome king who possessed unimaginable power. Anyone fortunate enough to enter their halls would be granted a single wish.
        
        Gold.
        
        Jewels.
        
        Knowledge.
        
        Power.
        
        Anything the heart desired. Yet there was a price to this miracle. The moment a visitor chose their reward, the Grand Mansion would disappear without a trace. The treasure and the person who found it
        
        would be the only proof that the legend had ever been real.
        
        Because of this, many considered it nothing more than a fairy tale. A story told to children before bedtime. A fantasy whispered around campfires. But legends have a strange way of hiding the truth.
        
        Far from the great cities and crowded marketplaces stood a small village nestled between rolling hills and ancient woods.
        
        Among its people lived a boy named Elias. Unlike the adventurers who dreamed of finding the Grand Mansion, Elias had little interest in treasure or riches. Life in the village was simple.
        
        Each morning, he helped his grandfather gather firewood, fetched water from the river, and tended the small vegetable garden behind their cottage.
        
        At sixteen years old, he had never traveled beyond the neighboring towns. Nor did he wish to. The world beyond the village seemed far too large and complicated.
        
        He was perfectly content with the life he already had. Or so he believed.
        
        One evening, as the sun dipped below the horizon, Elias sat beside the fireplace while his grandfather repaired an old fishing net. The old man suddenly chuckled.
        
        "Do you know," he said, "when I was your age, I dreamed of finding the Grand Mansion." Elias smiled. "That old legend again?" "It's not merely a legend."
        
        His grandfather's eyes sparkled in the firelight. "At least, that's what my grandfather used to say." Elias laughed. "And did he find it?"
        
        "No." The old man smiled. "But he always believed someone would." The room fell silent. Outside, the wind rustled through the trees. For a brief moment, Elias wondered whether the stories could be true.
        
        Whether somewhere beyond the forests and mountains stood a grand mansion waiting for the right person. Then he shook his head. Such things belonged in stories. Not in real life. Little did he know,
        
        fate had already begun leading him toward the greatest secret in the kingdom. And before long, he would discover that some legends are waiting for someone brave enough to believe in them.
