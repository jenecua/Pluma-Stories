The Transfer Student

Author: R.J.C.

Chapter 1: The Introduction


        It was the first day of the second semester. Clarisse walked into her school, scanning the room for a vacant chair. She spotted one and quickly sat down. Moments later, a transfer student arrived and took
        
        the seat beside her—the only one left.
        
        “Hi,” Clarisse greeted warmly. The transferee only gave a small nod. Just then, the professor entered, and the class began.
        
        Halfway through the lecture, Clarisse realized she had forgotten her pen. She leaned toward the transfer student. “Excuse me, may I borrow a pen?” she asked softly.
        
        The girl didn’t respond, so Clarisse tapped her shoulder. The transferee turned, stared at her for a moment, then silently pulled a pen from her bag and handed it over. “Thank you. By the way, I’m Clarisse.
        
        What’s your name?” she asked with a sweet smile.
        
        The girl met her gaze. “Raya,” she replied. “Hi, Raya. Nice to meet you,” Clarisse said cheerfully.
        
        When the discussion ended, it was already break time. Raya began packing her things, but Clarisse stood up and offered, “Come on, let’s eat together.”
        
        Raya looked at her with the same serious expression and gave a small nod. Clarisse laughed. “You’re too serious, Raya. Smile!” she teased. Raya forced herself to smile, though it was faint.
        
        Months passed. Clarisse talked endlessly—sharing stories, news, and little updates about her life—while Raya mostly listened in silence. By the end of the semester, the two had become inseparable.
        
        A year later, another transfer student joined the class. Her name was Harper, and she happened to be Clarisse’s childhood friend.
        
        The two were very close, treating each other like sisters. The moment Clarisse saw Harper, she jumped from her seat, ran to her, and hugged her tightly. Raya watched them, her jaw tightening.
        
        She felt a surge of anger. Clarisse was hers—no one else should take her away. Clarisse grabbed Harper’s hand and introduced her to Raya. “This is Harper. We grew up together. She’s like a sister to me.”
        
        Raya’s mood darkened. Jealousy gnawed at her. She wanted Harper gone. After class, the three walked home together. Raya trailed behind, head bowed, silent. Clarisse chatted nonstop with Harper,
        
        who laughed at everything she said.
        
        When they arrived home, Raya went straight to her bedroom. She threw her bag onto the bed and muttered bitterly, “We’ve been together for a whole semester, and now she ignores me because of Harper?”
        
        A voice answered. “Would you like me to remove her from the scene?” Startled, Raya turned toward the sound. It came from the oval mirror across the room. Her reflection stared back,
        
        but it wasn’t just her—it was
        
        another version of herself. “Don’t you want to be with Clarisse forever?” the reflection whispered.
        
        Raya froze. She realized she was talking to herself… but it wasn’t the same self. It was another side of her, a darker personality, one that wanted Clarisse all to herself.
