Elemental

Author: R.J.C.

Chapter 1: Revelation


        In a world hidden alongside the human world lives a secret race known as the Elementals. For centuries, they have existed in secrecy, blending in with ordinary people while possessing the power
        
        to control the four elements: Fire, Water, Earth, and Air. Using their elemental abilities, they build, create, protect, and survive.
        
        Their greatest rule is simple:
        
        Never reveal your powers to humans.
        
        Sixteen-year-old Aria has always followed that rule.
        
        But everything changes one afternoon when she accidentally uses her elemental magic in front of her closest human friend to save them from an accident.
        
        Instead of gratitude, she is met with fear.

        "You're a freak!" her friend cries before running away.
        
        The next day, rumors spread across the village.
        
        The frightened friend tells their parents, who tell their neighbors. Soon, the entire village believes the Elementals are dangerous monsters.
        
        Fear turns into hatred.
        
        Hatred turns into violence.
        
        As the villagers begin hunting those with elemental powers, Aria and her family are forced into hiding.
        
        But not everyone believes the lies.
        
        With tensions rising between humans and Elementals, Aria must uncover the truth behind a centuries-old secret that threatens both worlds.
        
        Who will believe them?
        
        Who will protect them?
        
        And what happens when a hidden world is no longer willing to remain hidden?
