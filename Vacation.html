Vacation

Author: R.J.C.

Chapter 1: Paradise Island


        "Come on, it's been years since we had a vacation together." Adrian smiled as he placed two plane tickets on the table. Across from him, his best friend, Noah, laughed. "You've been asking me for months.
        
        Fine. Let's go." Adrian nodded. Inside, he smiled for a completely different reason. The island wasn't meant to be a vacation. It was meant to be a grave.
        
        For years, Adrian had watched Noah succeed at everything. Noah had the better job, the better house, and even the woman Adrian secretly loved.
        
        Everyone admired Noah. No one noticed Adrian standing in his shadow. Until one day, envy became hatred. And hatred became a plan.
        
        A week later, they arrived at a private island several hours away from civilization. White sand beaches stretched endlessly along the shore. Palm trees swayed peacefully in the wind. It looked perfect.
        
        Isolated. Exactly what Adrian wanted. The first two days were normal. They explored the island, went fishing, and shared drinks at night. Noah never suspected a thing. Why would he?
        
        Adrian had been his best friend for over ten years. On the third night, they hiked to a cliff overlooking the ocean. The waves crashed against the rocks below.
        
        A single step in the wrong direction would be fatal. Noah stared at the moonlit water. "This place is amazing," he said. Adrian slowly approached him from behind.
        
        His heart pounded. This was the moment. Years of jealousy had led him here. All he had to do was push. Just one push. Adrian raised his trembling hands. Then Noah spoke.
        
        "You know, Adrian, you're the closest thing I have to a brother." Adrian froze. For a brief second, guilt pierced through the darkness in his mind. But only for a second.
        
        Adrian lowered his hands, his heart wavering for the first time that night. Noah's words echoed in his mind, stirring memories of their years of friendship. For a brief moment, he wondered if he should
        
        abandon the plan and tell Noah the truth. But the image of Noah's successful life soon reignited the bitterness buried deep within him. Clenching his fists, Adrian took another silent step forward,
        
        determined to finish what he had come to the island to do.
