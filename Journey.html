Journey

Author: R.J.C.

Chapter 1: The Vanishing Map


        Eighteen-year-old Ethan Carter struggled to make ends meet. After losing both of his parents, he worked long hours at a small bakery just to survive.
        
        One rainy evening, while walking home, Ethan noticed an old man sitting on a bench. The man appeared weak and hungry.
        
        "Young man," the stranger said, "could you spare some food?"
        
        Although the loaf of bread in Ethan's bag was the only meal he had for the night, he handed it to the old man without hesitation. The stranger smiled.

        In return, he gave Ethan an ancient map covered with strange symbols. "What is this?" Ethan asked. Before the old man could answer, both the map and the stranger vanished into thin air. Startled, Ethan
        
        dropped to the ground. Then something impossible happened. The symbols from the map appeared on his hand before fading away.
        
        Moments later, Ethan found himself standing on the other side of the city. He had teleported.

        As Ethan learns to control his newfound ability, he discovers that the map was no ordinary artifact. It was a legendary relic created by an ancient
        
        civilization known as the Pathkeepers, beings capable of traveling anywhere in the world with a single thought.
        
        But Ethan is not the only one searching for the map's power.
        
        Dark forces, treasure hunters, and a mysterious organization have spent centuries searching for the relic.
        
        Now that the map has chosen him, Ethan must uncover its secrets before they do.
