Mother's Love

Author: R.J.C.

Chapter 1: The Wrong Child


        Sarah Bennett stood motionless in the doorway of her son's bedroom. The boy sat on the floor, quietly building a tower from colorful blocks.
        
        Sunlight streamed through the window, illuminating his smiling face. A face she no longer recognized.
        
        Three months. That was how long her son, Oliver, had been missing. Three months of sleepless nights. Three months of crying herself to sleep. Three months of praying that someone, somewhere,
        
        would bring her little boy back.
        
        And then, two weeks ago, the police had found him. The entire town had celebrated. Her husband had cried. The news called it a miracle. Everyone believed the nightmare was over. Everyone except Sarah.
        
        The boy looked up and smiled. "Mommy, look what I made. His voice was familiar. His eyes were familiar. Everything about him should have been familiar. Yet Sarah felt nothing but dread.
        
        She forced a smile. "That's nice." The child returned to his toys. Sarah continued staring. Watching. Studying. Searching.
        
        Ever since Oliver's return, small details had haunted her. The way he held his spoon. The way he tied his shoes. The way he laughed. They were tiny things. The kind of things nobody else would notice.
        
        But she noticed. A mother always notices. And every day, the feeling grew stronger.
        
        This wasn't her son. "Where is he?" she whispered. The boy looked up. "What?" Sarah stepped closer. "Where is my real son?" The child's smile disappeared. His eyes widened with confusion. "Mommy?"
        
        "Stop calling me that." Fear appeared on the boy's face.
        
        A moment later, footsteps rushed down the hallway. Sarah's husband, Daniel, entered the room. "Sarah!" He immediately pulled her away. "What are you doing?" She pointed at the child.
        
        "Tell him to stop lying." Daniel stared at her in disbelief. "Sarah..." "That's not Oliver." The room fell silent. The boy began crying. Daniel's expression hardened. "We've talked about this."
        
        "No," Sarah said firmly. "You've talked. Nobody listens to me." "He's our son."
        
        "Then tell me why he feels different." Daniel rubbed his forehead. "Because he was kidnapped for three months." Sarah shook her head. "No." "Sarah..." "No!" she screamed. The frightened child covered
        
        his ears. Tears streamed down his face.
        
        Sarah looked at him carefully. The eyes. The voice. The smile. Everything was there. Everything except the feeling.The bond. The certainty. Gone.
        
        Replaced by a terrible conviction she could not explain. Someone had taken her child. And someone else had been brought back. That night, long after Daniel and the boy had fallen asleep, Sarah sat
        
        alone in the kitchen. Rain tapped softly against the windows. She opened the photo album she kept hidden inside a cabinet. Page after page showed pictures of Oliver growing up. Birthdays. School events.
        
        Family vacations.
        
        Sarah ran her fingers across one photograph. Then she froze. A chill traveled down her spine. Her eyes narrowed. She quickly turned on the lamp and brought the picture closer. There was something she
        
        had never noticed before. Something so small that anyone else would miss it. But not a mother. Not her. For the first time since Oliver's return, Sarah felt certain of one thing. She wasn't imagining
        
        it. And if the photograph was telling the truth… The child sleeping upstairs wasn't her son at all.
