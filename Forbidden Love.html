Forbidden Love

Author: R.J.C.

Chapter 1: Two Worlds


        The annual International Cultural Festival was the biggest event in the city.
        
        People from different countries gathered to celebrate their traditions, food, music, and history. Colorful decorations filled the streets, and the air buzzed with excitement.
        
        Among the crowd stood Michael Ramos, a young man from a proud and respected family originally from the Philippines.
        
        His parents had always been strict about one thing. Family tradition. Since childhood, Michael had heard the same words countless times. "When you marry, it must be someone from our own people."
        
        His family believed that preserving their culture and heritage was more important than anything else.
        
        Michael never argued. Until now. Across the festival grounds, Sophia Laurent adjusted a display at her family's cultural booth.
        
        Sophia came from a wealthy family whose roots traced back to another country. Like Michael's parents, her family also valued tradition above all else.
        
        Her father often reminded her of her responsibility. "One day, you will marry someone who understands our culture and our way of life." Sophia had always nodded obediently.
        
        But secretly, she wondered why love needed permission. As the afternoon sun warmed the festival grounds, fate brought the two together. Michael turned a corner while carrying a tray of drinks.
        
        At the same moment, Sophia stepped backward without looking. They collided. The drinks fell. Ice scattered across the pavement. "Oh no!" Sophia exclaimed. "I'm sorry," Michael said.
        
        Both spoke at the same time. Then they laughed. For a brief moment, everything around them seemed to disappear. The loud music. The crowded streets.  The bustling festival. All Michael could see
        
        was Sophia.
        
        And all Sophia could see was Michael. Michael bent down to collect the fallen cups. Sophia helped him. Their hands accidentally touched. Both quickly pulled away. Embarrassed smiles appeared on
        
        their faces. "I think we both share the blame," Sophia said. Michael chuckled. "I think you're right." For the next few minutes, they talked as if they had known each other for years.
        
        The conversation felt easy. Natural. Comfortable. Something neither of them expected. When Sophia's father called her from across the festival, she glanced back reluctantly. "I should go."
        
        Michael nodded. "Me too." For a moment, neither moved. Neither wanted the conversation to end. "Maybe we'll see each other again," Michael said. Sophia smiled. "Maybe."
       
        As they walked away, neither realized that several people had been watching. Michael's mother watched from a distance with concern. Sophia's father watched with suspicion.
        
        Both had noticed the same thing.
        
        The spark between their children.
        
        And if there was one thing both families agreed on, it was this:
        
        That relationship could never happen. Yet as evening settled over the city, Michael found himself thinking about Sophia.
        
        And Sophia found herself smiling whenever she remembered Michael's laugh. Neither knew it yet. But a single accidental meeting was about to challenge generations of tradition.
        
        And soon, they would have to answer a painful question:
        
        Was love worth risking everything for?
